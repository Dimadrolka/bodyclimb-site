How long should you hold a plank?

Hold it as long as your body stays in one straight line, and stop when your hips sag or lift and will not come back. Bodyclimb's working target is 3 sets of 30 to 45 seconds, with your knees on the mat if you need an easier version. How long to hold, and what the numbers say

Bodyclimb's working target for the plank is 3 sets of 30 to 45 seconds with a full, straight line. Once you reach it, Bodyclimb's next steps make the position harder rather than the plank longer: the side plank and the hollow hold. Your own time is the number that counts: write it down and compare only with your own last result.

For context only, here is one study. Strand et al. (2014) measured a forearm plank held to failure in 471 US college students, about 20 years old. For men, the 10th, 50th and 90th percentiles were 62, 110 and 201 seconds. For women they were 35, 72 and 142 seconds (Strand et al. 2014). These are results from young students tested to failure, not targets for you, and we found no study that gives plank norms for adults by age.

How to do a plank

  1. Kneel on a mat and put your forearms down, elbows right under your shoulders. Forearms point straight ahead, shoulder-width apart, palms flat.
  2. Step your feet back one at a time onto the balls of your feet, legs straight.
  3. Lift your knees so your shoulders, hips and heels make one straight line.
  4. Pull your lower ribs toward your hips and squeeze your bottom.
  5. Press your forearms into the floor and look at the mat between your hands.
  6. Hold still and keep breathing. Lower your knees to the mat when the time is up.

You should feel your stomach working the most, with your bottom and the front of your thighs helping. There should be no pinch in the lower back and no strain in the neck.

Common mistakes

  • Hips sagging. Squeeze your bottom and pull your ribs toward your hips until the line is straight again.
  • Hips piked up. Lower your hips until they are level with your shoulders and heels.
  • Holding your breath. Take slow, quiet breaths. Come down sooner if you can only hold on by straining.

When to stop

Pain means stop. End the plank if your lower back or a shoulder hurts, or if your hips drop and will not come back up. With a sore shoulder, put your hands on a kitchen counter with straight arms instead.

If it is too hard today

Keep your knees on the mat, or put your hands on a kitchen counter with straight arms. Hold the same straight line. If your back lifts or sags, the dead bug is the step before the plank in Bodyclimb, and the bird dog is another early core move.

Questions

How long should a beginner hold a plank?

Hold it as long as your body stays in one straight line. Bodyclimb's working target is 3 sets of 30 to 45 seconds. If that is too long, use shorter holds, or keep your knees on the mat.

Is there an average plank time?

One study of 471 US college students, about 20 years old, found median forearm plank times to failure of 110 s for men and 72 s for women (Strand et al. 2014). That describes young students tested to failure. It is not a target.

Is a longer plank better?

Not for moving on. Once you hold 3 sets of 30 to 45 seconds in a straight line, Bodyclimb's next steps make the position harder, a side plank and a hollow hold, rather than the plank longer. A longer plank is fine as a test while your line stays straight.

Should I plank on my forearms or my hands?

Bodyclimb's plank is on your forearms, elbows right under your shoulders. With a sore shoulder, put your hands on a kitchen counter with straight arms.

Why does my lower back hurt in a plank?

Your hips are probably sagging. Squeeze your bottom, pull your ribs toward your hips and end the hold when the line breaks. If the back pain stays, stop.

What if I feel pain?

Stop. Pain in your lower back or a shoulder means end the plank. Pain means stop.
