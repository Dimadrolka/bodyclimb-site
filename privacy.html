Bodyclimb

Privacy Policy

The same words you can read inside the app.

In short

Bodyclimb has no account and no sign-in. What you enter stays on this phone. The app sends nothing about you anywhere: no analytics, no ads, no tracking, no third parties.

What the app keeps, on this phone

Your answers (birth year, the health check, your gear, the days you train), your workouts and the settings you choose. The plan is worked out on the phone from them.

Health answers

The health check and pain notes are kept apart from your workouts, in storage the phone protects while it is locked, and are left out of the phone’s backups. They are never shared.

Backups

Your workouts are part of your phone’s own backup (iCloud or a computer), if you use one. Apple keeps that backup under your Apple Account; Bodyclimb cannot read it.

Purchases

Packs and Bodyclimb Full are bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; Bodyclimb never sees your payment details, only that a purchase is yours.

Reminders

Workout reminders are set on the phone itself. Nothing goes through a server.

Crash reports

If you turned on “Share with App Developers” in the iPhone’s settings, Apple may pass crash reports to us. They hold no workouts and no answers.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes everything it kept on this phone. A phone backup made before keeps its copy of your workouts until that backup is replaced or deleted.

Age

Bodyclimb is for ages 16 and up. A younger person can’t train in the app. Where the law asks for it, the app also asks your Apple Account for your age range and keeps only the band — under 16, 16–17 or 18 and over — on this phone.

If this changes

If Bodyclimb ever sends anything — for example anonymous statistics to improve the plans — the app will ask you first, and this policy will say exactly what is sent.

Contact

Bodyclimb is made by Dmytro Nour. Questions about this policy or your data: support@bodyclimb.app.
