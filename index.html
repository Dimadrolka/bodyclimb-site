Before you hang on

I can’t do a single pull-up. Is this really for me?

It was built for you. The pull-up line starts with a hang where your feet still touch the floor, and the first workouts are short.

Do I need a gym?

No. A doorway bar or a park bar covers the pull line; chest, legs and core need only the floor, a wall, a chair or a table. Tell the app what you have and the workout is built from it. No bar today? A towel shut in a sturdy door is a row.

How long until my first pull-up?

Honestly, it depends on where you start, and for most adults it is months, not days. The app shows a range for your next step, never a promise, and narrows it as it learns how you train.

How long is a workout?

You choose: from about 15 minutes to an hour, on the days you pick. Several directions can train together in one workout.

What if something hurts?

Say so during the workout and that area gets rest; “Unwell” stops the workout at once. Bodyclimb is a training plan, not medical advice: if a doctor has told you to be careful with exercise, ask them first.

What happens to my data?

Your workouts stay on your iPhone. No account, no ads, no tracking, and the app works offline. This website sets no cookies either.
