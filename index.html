It sees every rep. In the next update

Put your iPhone down and train. The camera counts your reps and times your holds, so your hands stay on the bar, not on the screen.

  1. Lean it, step back.

    Next to each exercise, a picture shows where your phone reads you best. For rows, about two metres to the side.

  2. Every rep, at your pace.

    Reps from how your joints move, holds from the shape of your body, on the floor and on the bar. Fast or slow, the count keeps up.

    One hand over your head ends the set. The rest runs by itself, and after it the workout moves on without a tap.

    When a count may be off, the app tells you why and asks. Fix it with − and + before it goes into your plan.

      The Heights

    Times are ranges, never promises. Most adults need months, not days, to reach the first pull-up, and that’s fine: a step you have reached stays reached, and a hard day says “Not yet”, never “Failed”.

    Where would your climb start?

    Three questions, the same ones the app asks for Strong back, read by the same table. No bar needed. Answer from the sofa.

    The app asks a little more and checks you in the first workouts, so treat this as a first look.

    How many strict pull-ups can you do in a row?

    From a full hang to chin over the bar, no swing. Count the ones you are sure of.

    How long can you hold your chin over the bar?

    Step up from a chair, then hold the top with bent arms.

    How long can you hang from a bar?

    Straight arms, full grip, feet off the floor.

    Before you hang on

    I can’t do a single pull-up. Is this really for me?

    It was built for you. The pull-up line starts with a hang where your feet still touch the floor, the push-up line with a push-up against the wall, and the first workouts are short.

    Do I need a gym?

    No. A doorway bar or a park bar covers the pull line; chest, legs and core need only the floor, a wall, a chair or a table. Tell the app what you have and the workout is built from it. No bar today? A towel shut in a sturdy door is a row.

    Does the camera record me?

    The camera comes in the next update, and it will not record you. While you train, your iPhone reads where your joints are and counts from that. Video is never stored or sent. You can turn the camera off at any time and log your reps yourself.

    Is the coach an AI?

    The coach’s chat, voice and camera come in the next update. The chat is: its answers come from an AI model by Anthropic, through Bodyclimb’s own server. It answers from your plan and the app’s exercise guides, it can be wrong, and nothing in your plan changes until you tap Do it. The chat is for adults (18+), needs a connection and has a daily limit. Your pain notes and health answers are never sent to it. Leo’s and Ava’s voices and the workout music were made with AI ahead of time and play from your iPhone. The camera reads your joints with a pose model that runs on your iPhone.

    How long until my first pull-up?

    Honestly, it depends on where you start, and for most adults it is months, not days. The app shows a range for your next step, never a promise, and narrows it as it learns how you train.

    How long is a workout?

    You choose: from about 15 minutes to an hour, on the days you pick. Several directions can train together in one workout.

    What if something hurts?

    Say so during the workout and that area gets rest; “Unwell” stops the workout at once. Bodyclimb is a training plan, not medical advice: if a doctor has told you to be careful with exercise, ask them first.

    What happens to my data?

    Your workouts are kept on your iPhone. No account, no ads, no tracking. If you use the coach chat (next update), each question goes to the AI with a short summary of your training, only to answer it; the Privacy Policy lists exactly what is sent and who keeps it for how long.
