Support

Help with training, purchases and your data.

Developer: Dmytro Nour

Write to us: support@bodyclimb.app

We read every message. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you use.

Restore your purchases

Packs and Bodyclimb Full belong to your Apple Account. On a new iPhone open Profile → Restore purchases.

Manage or cancel Bodyclimb Full yearly

Open Profile → Manage subscription in the app, or Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to stop the next renewal. A free trial turns into the subscription at its end unless you cancel.

Refunds

Purchases are billed and refunded by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Bodyclimb has no account and sends nothing about you. Everything stays on your iPhone; deleting the app deletes it. See the Privacy Policy.

Safety

Bodyclimb is a training plan, not medical advice. If a doctor has told you to be careful with exercise, ask them before you train. Stop when something hurts or you feel unwell. Bodyclimb is for ages 16 and up.
