Plank Test

  1. Set up. Forearms on the floor, elbows under your shoulders, knees down. Put your phone where you can see it.
  2. Hold. Tap Start. When the countdown ends, lift your knees: shoulders, hips and heels in one straight line. Keep breathing.
  3. Stop when your form breaks: your hips sag or lift and won’t come back. Lower your knees, then tap Stop.

Nothing starts until you tap Start.
