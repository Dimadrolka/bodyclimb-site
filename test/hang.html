Self-timed test

Dead Hang Test

  1. Set up. A solid bar, and a box or chair so you can reach it without jumping.
  2. Hang. Tap Start and take a full grip. When the countdown ends, step off and hang on straight arms.
  3. Stop when your form breaks: your fingers start to open, or you can’t stay still. Step back onto the box, then tap Stop.

Nothing starts until you tap Start.
