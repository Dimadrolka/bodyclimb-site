Not medical advice

Bodyclimb is a training plan, not medical advice, a diagnosis or treatment. If a doctor has told you to be careful with exercise, or you have a heart condition, chest pain, dizziness or an injury, ask your doctor before you train. Until a doctor clears you, the app keeps to gentle steps: the hardest steps and max tests stay off.

Your training, your call

You train at your own risk. Warm up, keep to clean form, and stop when something hurts or you feel unwell — the app’s “Unwell” button stops the workout at once. Chest pain or trouble breathing that doesn’t pass needs medical help right away.
